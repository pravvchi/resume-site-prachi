More Than One Perspective.

First-year MBBS student at KLE Medical College, concurrently pursuing a BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship at IIMB. Drawn to the space where medicine and business meet, particularly public health, health-tech and healthcare management. Curious, determined and sincere, with a strong moral compass in both professional and personal life, and an eagerness to learn, plan and execute.

Who I Am

I am a student pursuing two demanding yet deeply complementary fields simultaneously: an MBBS degree at KLE Medical College in Belagavi and a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB).

Rather than viewing these as separate academic paths, I see them as twin pillars of a cohesive journey. Preclinical medical training instills empirical discipline, scientific rigor, and empathy for human fragility, while business education provides tools of strategic management, operational optimization, and systemic problem-solving.

What Drives Me

My primary motivation stems from an innate curiosity about how complex systems interact with human welfare. At one end of the spectrum is the human body—a marvel of intricate biological coordination. At the other end are our healthcare delivery mechanisms—hospitals, clinics, distribution chains, and digital networks—which frequently suffer from institutional bottlenecks, inefficiencies, and access disparities.

I am driven by the belief that the next generation of healthcare leaders must be capable of understanding both domains. It is not enough to diagnose an illness if the medicine cannot be delivered efficiently to those who need it most; nor is it effective to build healthcare technology without deep empathy for the clinicians and patients who actually use it.

Why Medicine and Business?

The healthcare ecosystem is undergoing unprecedented structural changes. The advent of artificial intelligence, remote diagnostics, digital patient records, and value-based healthcare models requires physicians who can communicate seamlessly with technologists, policymakers, and business executives.

By grounding myself in medicine at KLE Medical College and digital business at IIM Bangalore, I am deliberately building the capacity to navigate both worlds. Whether it involves public health initiatives, medical startup entrepreneurship, or hospital governance, this dual-curriculum path allows me to approach problems with clinical sensitivity and operational precision.

My Approach to Learning

My educational philosophy is anchored in four core values: determination, sincerity, ethical judgement, and an insatiable eagerness to learn, plan, and execute.

I believe in rigorous, meticulous preparation—setting clear goals and executing them with discipline—while maintaining the intellectual flexibility to welcome spontaneous discoveries and unexpected creative insights. Across my academic pursuits and creative endeavors, I hold firmly to an unwavering moral compass, ensuring that empathy and ethical integrity always remain at the core of every endeavor.

Balancing Discipline and Spontaneity

Pursuing an MBBS concurrently with a BBA requires an extraordinary degree of deliberate organization. The preclinical medical curriculum demands daily retention of complex anatomical systems, pharmacological mechanisms, and biological pathways.

However, rigid structure without spontaneous curiosity leads to burnout. By engaging in street theatre, debating, contemporary dance, and multidisciplinary case studies, I cultivate the creative elasticity needed to look at clinical problems with fresh, compassionate eyes.

“Carefully planned milestones provide the scaffolding; spontaneous creativity provides the insight.”
