The Philosophical Foundation

Why Two Disciplines?

I am dedicated to becoming a physician who understands the systems that deliver care as deeply as the patients who depend on them. Pursuing an MBBS alongside a BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship at IIMB is a deliberate choice: I am genuinely passionate about both fields and about the common ground they share, from medical entrepreneurship to health-tech.

I want to learn how healthcare is organised and managed, and how technology and enterprise can widen access to quality care, with a particular pull towards public health and healthcare management. I bring determination, sincerity and a strong moral compass to this path, and I am eager to build, lead and contribute to healthcare ventures that put patients first.

Strategic Pillars

Three Horizons of Impact

Rather than treating medicine as an isolated clinical silo, my future aspirations are structured across three interconnected domains:

HORIZON 01

Public Health

A profound interest in improving access to healthcare and understanding population-level healthcare challenges. Investigating systemic health inequities, preventive medicine strategies, and community-centered interventions tailored to diverse socioeconomic contexts.

HORIZON 02

Health-Tech

Exploring how technology and digital enterprise can widen access to quality clinical care. Focusing on AI-assisted diagnostics, telehealth networks, remote patient monitoring, and interoperable health data infrastructure that bridges urban-rural divides.

HORIZON 03

Healthcare Management

Understanding how healthcare institutions, clinics, and health systems are organized, governed, and managed. Designing efficient hospital workflows, optimizing medical logistics, and creating humane, resilient institutional environments for clinicians and patients alike.

Core Guiding Commitment

“I want to build, lead and contribute to healthcare ventures that put patients first.”

An aspiration grounded in scientific rigor, elevated by enterprise thinking, and steered by an unwavering ethical compass.
