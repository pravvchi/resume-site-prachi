Nukkad Natak: Street Theatre for Social Consciousness

As the winning street play performance, this production moved beyond traditional entertainment to confront high-friction societal realities right in the middle of public spaces.

Core Social Themes Investigated:

  • Abuse Against Doctors: Confronting the alarming rise of physical and verbal hostility directed at healthcare professionals, highlighting physician vulnerability and the need for structural security.
  • Gender Equality: Deconstructing everyday micro-biases, domestic imbalances, and institutional glass ceilings.
  • Regressive Attention Spans: Addressing digital addiction and algorithmic fragmentation affecting community empathy and focus.
  • Desensitisation & Selective Outrage: Questioning the collective numbness toward everyday suffering contrasted with transient viral outcries.
